How to Buy WDM

A simple step-by-step guide for first-time users.

Step 1. Prepare a Crypto Wallet

Install a compatible wallet such as MetaMask and create or use your wallet.

Step 2. Select the Base Network

Open your wallet and select the Base network.

WDM is issued on the Base network.

Step 3. Prepare ETH on Base

You need ETH on the Base network to swap for WDM and to pay network gas fees.

If you transfer ETH from an exchange, select Base as the withdrawal network.

Step 4. Open Uniswap

Open Uniswap and connect your wallet.

Make sure your wallet is connected to the Base network.

Step 5. Find WDM

Search for WDM and check the official contract address.

0x4C154CaF238efD0811e15D9b30d074358F6468D1

WDM is the token with this contract address.

Step 6. 매수 금액 입력

Enter the amount of ETH you want to swap for WDM.

For your first transaction, you can start with a small amount.

Step 7. 거래 내용 확인

Check the WDM amount, ETH amount, price impact, slippage and network fee.

Step 8. Confirm the Swap

If the transaction details are correct, confirm the swap in your wallet.

Wait until the transaction is confirmed on the Base network.

Step 9. Check Your WDM Balance

After the transaction is confirmed, check your wallet to make sure the WDM has been received.

Important

Cryptocurrency prices and network fees can change. Please check the transaction details before confirming your swap.
